# Beat chart v1 (approved 2026-09-24)

What happens where, and roughly when, along the routes of `map_plan_v1.png`. The rule:
**something interesting every 2-3 minutes** (a place, a landmark, a puzzle, wildlife,
a story moment, a new mechanic). Times are from the start for a first-time pair of
players, driving plus stops. Puzzle details are sketches; each gets its own design
page before it is built (Milestones C-F).

Legend: **[teach]** a mechanic is taught · **[story]** · **[puzzle]** ·
**[place]** a landmark worth the stop · **[life]** wildlife / world moment ·
**[mood]** colour, weather, traffic change

| 1:30 | [teach] Town road: other cars, keep left, don't hit anyone. [life] Cows crossing. | [teach] Fill a can from the fuel drum in the shed (pouring); find the half coolant jug. |

## The way out (6:00 - 25:00), bright, busy roads, slowly greying

| Time | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| 6:30 | P2's home -> J1 | [life] Birds lift off the road ahead; oncoming traffic. |
| 7:30 | **J1 Windmill** | [story] Old text from Naresh. [teach] + [puzzle] **Windmill**: one climbs the tower, one works the brake lever below; the climber **tags** where to go (teaches tagging). Frees the blades. Reward: the map of this side of the valley. |
| 8:30 | Road choice | **Valley (long)**: Mirror Lake dock (Memory Fragment, [story] Naresh's bench), the barn ([puzzle] optional: spare coolant + a crate stack). **Ridge (short)**: steeper, hotter engine; the lookout ([teach] **binoculars**: spot the water works and the bridge), the wreck ([story] "our" tent, one sleeping bag). |
| 11:30 | **J2 Last Fuel** | [teach] refuel from cans (pumps dead). [mood] **The last other car turns back here**; after this the road is yours alone. [story] Naresh's text. |
| 12:30 | Pump House Road | [teach] the hose splits: overheating. |
| 13:30 | **Water works** | [puzzle] Valves + pump (exists). Getting it running **also restarts the old turbine**: power flows to the bridge (cause and effect). |
| 16:00 | **The bridge** (the gate) | [puzzle] A lift bridge stuck up. With the power back, one runs the control hut, one clears the jammed gear on the deck, guided by tags. It comes down. |
| 18:00 | Ghat hairpins | [mood] clouds, first grey. [teach] **First creature**, glimpsed in the trees: it comes for the van, the fuel starts to leak. Pull over, **camouflage tarp**, engine off, peek until it goes. |
| 21:00 | **Coast watchtower** | [place] First sight of the sea and Bessi beach far off. [teach] **Hiding on foot**: a creature patrols the base; behind cover, lean to peek, the cardboard box. [teach] Stamp the beach on the map; the nav follows your stamp. |
| 23:00 | Down to the coast | [life] Casuarina groves, wind, gulls. [mood] greyer, dusk. |
| 24:00 | **Bessi beach** | [place] Empty promenade, stall lights on, boats, the memorial. [puzzle] **The photo**: find where Naresh's photo was taken (binoculars + tags). [story] Smoke rolls in; **the Five Roses rise**; Naresh in the fifth. [story] Evidence there was never a friend. |

## With Naresh (27:00 - 55:00), no more guidance

| Time | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| 27:00 | The beach | [teach] 1-2 short, fun puzzles showing Naresh's commands and his random acts (good and bad). Then **the storm hits**: the way you came is a wall of weather. |
| 31:00 | Coast road north | [mood] dark, rain. Creatures now follow Naresh. |
| 33:00 | **Fishing village** | [puzzle] Fuel is in a locked net shed: use Naresh as a decoy to lead creatures away while one player searches. Naresh may wander (keep him between you). |
| 37:00 | **Salt pans** | [puzzle] Wide open, no cover: cross in stages, hiding the van, timing the creature's patrol. |
| 40:00 | **Estuary bridge** | [puzzle] Three people needed (two players + Naresh on a command). |
| 44:00 | **Old rail tunnel** | [place] Underground and dark: torches, batteries. Just off the path: the hidden **flare gun**. |
| 48:00 | **Radio mast** | [puzzle] The final, bigger puzzle using everything learnt. |
| 52:00 | **Naresh's home** | [story] Mother and sister; the light returns. Hidden **tracker** if every optional puzzle was done. |

## The drive home (55:00 - 60:00), full sun

| Time | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| 55:00 | West road | [mood] the most beautiful the game gets: sun, birds, wind in the grass. |
| 58:00 | **Ending watchtower** | [place] Every puzzle site of the journey visible from the top. |
| 59:00 | Nearly home | [story] Phone: "Naresh has left his house, heading to LiveStander". End. |

Target: about 60 minutes for a first play (longer for players who explore and do the
optional puzzles). No gap between interesting moments is longer than about 3 minutes.
