# The return and the ending (Milestone F): design v1 (proposal)

One page, for approval. Follows the story arc (`DESIGN.md` section 2, beats 8-10),
the beat chart (31:00-60:00), `FUTURE.md` #23 (the return feels like fear,
irritation and confusion, still fun and clever; three-person puzzles; a big final
puzzle; the watchtower look-back), `design/CREATURES.md` and `design/NARESH.md`.

## Goal

**Twist everything you learnt.** The way out taught one mechanic per place; the
return combines them, adds Naresh as an unreliable third pair of hands, and puts
pressure on it from the world: dark, rain, creatures that follow Naresh. No more
guidance (no objective line; the map and your memory). Then the relief: the
light comes back near Naresh's home, and the drive home is the most beautiful
the game gets.

## Where

Bessi -> Coast Road north (fishing village, salt pans, estuary bridge) -> under
Tunnel Hill (the old rail tunnel) -> the radio mast -> Naresh's home -> West Road
home, with the Tower Road branch past the ending watchtower to P2's. About 29
minutes. The storm sits over the ghat (the way you came).

## The storm road (the old way)

- The ghat and the Beach Road are under the storm: heavy rain, fog, lightning, and
  **gusts** that push the van sideways and can tip it at speed.
- "Careful players can still get through" (`DESIGN.md`): slow down (under 25 km/h
  the gusts can't tip you), and it's passable.
- **Question for you** (see the end): where does it lead? Proposal: it's a real but
  brutal way back to J3. From there the **lift bridge is swinging open in the wind**
  (a world gate, not an invisible wall), so it's a dead end that costs fuel and time,
  and players who try it come back and take the coast road. A tired joke from
  Naresh when you return: "My friend said north."

## The finale: point the dishes home (radio mast)

Uses **everything**: power, tags, binoculars, cranks with Naresh, hiding, the van.

1. **Power** (cause and effect, like the water works): the mast's generator needs
   the **van's battery**. Drive the van to the base in the storm; its engine noise
   draws creatures (they're coming for Naresh anyway).
2. **Tags**: one climbs the mast (like the lookout, higher) and tags which of the
   three landmarks each dish must face: **the windmill, the water tower, the coast
   tower**, all visible from the top in lightning flashes.
3. **Cranks**: each dish turns with a crank at the base. Two players and **Naresh**
   each work one. His spins the wrong way until you tell him to *wait*.
4. **Binoculars**: each dish has a sight; a small screen at the base shows what the
   dish sees (the mirror tech). The climber confirms from above.
5. **Hiding**: halfway, two creatures come for the van. Tarp it (its battery is out
   anyway), go quiet, keep Naresh between you, or spend a flare. Then finish.

When the third dish lines up, the mast hums, the clouds over the West Road break,
and the rain stops there. That's the way out of the dark.

## The tracker (hidden 100% reward)

"Every optional puzzle" means: the barn maze (W2), the lookout relay (W4), the
tunnel's flare gun, plus every Memory Fragment that belongs to a puzzle (the
Bessi store batteries are a required step since E6; decision 4). Nothing on screen counts them (`DESIGN.md` 3).
The end watchtower at most hints: "some lights out there are still dark", and the
unlit sites are the ones you skipped.

## Storm gym (first)

A flat plain with a road, wind machines and a gust schedule. Fix: gust strength that
pushes the van 1-2 m sideways at 50 km/h, tips it above ~45 km/h on a crosswind,
never under 25 km/h; rain and fog visibility (~60 m); lightning flash lighting; how
the wipers and headlights help. Then the storm is placed over the ghat.

## Decisions (the user, 2026-10-01)

1. **The storm road is a brutal dead end.** Passable slowly (gusts can't tip the
   van under 25 km/h), but at J3 the lift bridge has swung open in the wind; you
   turn back to the coast road. Naresh on the way back: "My friend said north."
2. **The dishes point at nearer return sites, not the way-out landmarks.** The
   mast is 1.5-2.3 km from the windmill, the water works and the coast tower
   (draw distance 2.6 km, storm sight ~60 m), and there is no water tower in
   the world. Candidates: the estuary bridge, the tunnel portal, Naresh's home;
   the final three are picked by a sightline test from the mast top when it is
   built. The look back at the whole journey stays at the ending watchtower.
3. **His mother and sister say nothing about the friend.** The mother hugs him;
   the sister looks at the empty space beside him. Ambiguous, as agreed.
4. **The tracker counts:** the barn maze (W2), the lookout relay (W4), the
   tunnel's flare gun, and every puzzle Memory Fragment. The Bessi store
   batteries are dropped (a required step since E6).

5. **R1, the decoy (fishing village), agreed 2026-10-01:** two creatures pace
   between the huts and the net shed; on the return creatures drift towards
   Naresh. Send him to wait at the end of the jetty: they follow him out and
   the shed side clears. The shed's key is in a small boat on its flat roof
   (climb stacked fish crates). The watcher (binoculars, from the van's roof
   or a hut) calls him back from afar (look at him, V, Follow me) before one
   reaches him; taken, he's dropped high up 200-400 m away (time lost, no
   fail). The van arrives low on fuel (the lamp on near the village); the
   shed holds a full can. Leaving, he "refuels" with the empty one (the
   scripted mistake); the van dies ~300 m on and the creatures, following
   him, come while you pour the full can yourself.
