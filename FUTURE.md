# Future additions

Ideas agreed for later. Nothing here is in the current plan; these start **after**
milestones A–E of the 30-minute demo are finished.

## Professional-grade open world (after all milestones)

Expand the map from the demo's compact valley into a large, hand-authored world in
the style of Red Dead Redemption 2 and GTA V. The goal is a place that feels alive,
not just a road with scenery.

- **Encounter pacing.** Follow the rule those games use: every 1–2 minutes of travel,
  something interesting appears. Examples: a roadside event, a strange landmark, a
  wildlife moment, a stranded traveller, a view that opens up. Plan it deliberately
  along each route, not at random.
- **Living nature.** Ambient nature sound that changes by area (forest, open field,
  water, hills). Birds taking off and flying overhead, other wildlife, wind moving the
  trees and grass.
- **Full day/night cycle.** Sunrise, midday, sunset, night with moon and stars, and
  lighting and sky colour changing through the day.
- **Dynamic weather.** Changing climate: clear, overcast, rain, storms and fog, with
  matching sound and effects on driving (wet roads, visibility).
- **Bigger, denser map.** More regions, towns and points of interest, with distinct
  landmarks so the paper-map navigation stays rewarding at the larger scale.
