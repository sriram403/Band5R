# The puzzles: your play sheet

All 16 puzzles in journey order, so you can play each one and we can
discuss what to change, one at a time (your plan, 2026-10-05). Each one
has: how to get there with F1, the idea, the steps, and what you should
see. When you've played one, tell me its number and what you'd change.

Two players: P1 on keyboard + mouse, P2 on the controller, split screen.

**Every puzzle starts the same way:** start `Play.bat` and pick **New
game**. Press **F1**, go to the **Puzzles** tab (arrow keys), pick the
puzzle and press **Enter**, then press **F1** (or Esc) to close the menu.
This puts the puzzle back to how you first find it (also after you've done
it), sets the story to its step and puts you both there. From Bessi on it
gives you the binoculars and brings Naresh along.

**Controls**

| | P1 | P2 (pad) |
|---|---|---|
| Use / hold | E | X |
| Tag | T | RT |
| Binoculars, peek | hold RMB | hold LT |
| Crouch | Ctrl | B |
| Torch | F | Y |
| Naresh's jobs | V | D-pad Up |
| Map | M | D-pad Down |
| Phone | P | D-pad Right |
| Throw, fire a flare | G / LMB | RB |
| Swing the nav (in the van) | N | A |
| Hint | hold H | R3 |

## The way out

### 1. The water works

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **1. The water works**.

**The idea:** one player pumps and watches the pressure gauge. The other
sets two valves in the yard, so the water goes to the blue tank.

1. **Pumper:** at the hand pump by the pump house door, hold E. Keep the
   needle in the green.
   - **You should see:** the needle rises. If it goes too high, the relief
     valve bangs and the pump stops for a few seconds.
2. **Valve player:** follow the grey pipes by eye from valves A and B, and
   turn each valve (E / X) so the water reaches the **blue** tank.
   - **You should see:** the blue tank's level rises while the pumper
     pumps in the green. With a wrong setting, the grey tank fills or the
     water runs off to the river.
3. **Valve B slips twice** (it says "WORN SEAT" on its post).
   - **You should see:** a CLANK, B's pointer swings, and the needle falls.
     Turn B back and keep pumping.
4. **You should see:** when the tank is full, a coolant jug and a pink
   Memory Fragment come out of the tap. The turbine by the river starts
   turning, and the lamps light one by one along the road north.

*If stuck:* A points on to valve B, and B points to the blue tank.
Valve B slips for one player on the keyboard too (fixed 2026-10-05: it
used to slip only with P2 on a pad).

### 2. The windmill

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **2. The windmill**.

**The idea:** a rope is snagged on one blade. The climber up top tags that
blade. The player at the brake stops it at the bottom, so the climber can
cut the rope.

1. **Climber:** at the ladder on the back of the tower, press E and hold W.
   - **You should see:** you step off onto a platform under the blades.
2. **Climber:** look at the blade with the rope round it and tag it.
   - **You should see:** a pin on both screens, "the snagged blade". It
     moves with the blade.
3. **Other player:** at the red brake lever out front, press E to let the
   brake off. Press E again when the tagged blade reaches the bottom.
   - **You should see:** the blades creep round and stop. If they stop too
     early or too late, the climber is told "out of reach": try again.
4. **Climber:** hold E to cut the rope. **Other player:** brake off.
   - **You should see:** the blades spin up. A few seconds later, the
     miller's box at the foot springs open.
5. Take the map from the box and open your own map (M).
   - **You should see:** both roads to Last Fuel drawn in.
6. **Climber:** to come down, look at the top of the ladder, press E and
   hold S.

### 3. The barn maze (optional, Valley Road)

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **3. The barn maze**.

**The idea:** the guide on the loft balcony sees the whole maze. The
walker inside sees only hedges.

1. **Guide:** climb the ladder on the barn's west end to the balcony.
   **Walker:** go into the maze.
2. **Guide:** tag the turns for the walker.
3. **Walker:** in the middle of the maze, hay dust blows in.
   - **You should see:** the guide can't see into the dust. The walker
     describes what's near (a red gate, a scarecrow, a blue drum, a cart).
4. **Walker:** open the feed chest at the far end.
   - **You should see:** a coolant jug and a crate.

*Already agreed for later (on the polish list):* the guide stops a hay
blower in the loft, and a faint plan of the maze is pinned on the balcony.

### 4. The lookout boards (optional, Ridge Track)

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **4. The lookout boards**.

**The idea:** two boards across the valley show pictures you can only read
through binoculars. The box at the bottom has picture dials.

1. **Top player:** walk up the ramp and take the binoculars on the deck.
   Hold RMB / LT and look across the valley for the boards "1" and "2".
   - **You should see:** two pictures on each board (circle, square,
     triangle, cross, ring or diamond). By eye they are just pale dots.
2. **Bottom player:** at the green box at the foot of the ramp there are
   four dials, numbered 1-4 from left to right. E / X turns one. Board 1
   sets dials 1 and 2; board 2 sets dials 3 and 4.
   - **You should see:** a CLACK, the box opens, and there's a full fuel
     can inside.

### 5. The lift bridge

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **5. The lift bridge**. The water works counts as done, so the power is on.

**The idea:** the operator in the hut can only see the machinery through a
safety mirror. The partner across the road clears the jammed gear, then
rides the counterweight down.

1. **Operator:** walk up the little ramp into the hut. Hold E on LOWER.
   - **You should see:** the motor strains and the leaf shudders. A message
     says it's jammed. The mirror outside the window shows the machinery
     house.
2. **Partner:** walk out on the deck and into the machinery house across
   the road. Hold X on the wedge in the gear.
   - **You should see:** "Wedged tight... back it off (RAISE)".
3. **Operator:** hold RAISE while the partner holds X on the wedge.
   - **You should see:** after about 2.5 s the wedge drops into the river.
4. **Operator:** hold LOWER.
   - **You should see:** below about 30°, the cut-out trips and the leaf
     goes back up to 45°.
5. **Operator:** watch the mirror and let go of LOWER when the
   counterweight in the pit comes up level with the floor. **Partner:**
   step onto it. **Operator:** LOWER again.
   - **You should see:** the partner rides up, the leaf comes down and
     locks, and the barriers go. You can drive across.

### 6. The ghat pace notes

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **6. The ghat pace notes**. You start in the van at the foot of the ghat, P2 alongside.

**The idea:** fog on the hairpins. Only the passenger can read the bends
on the nav.

1. **P2:** swing the nav (A). Drive up the hill.
   - **You should see:** fog comes down. P2's nav shows notes like "70 LEFT
     HAIRPIN / DON'T CUT"; the driver's nav is blank. P2 calls them out.
2. On the second hairpin, look out to the side.
   - **You should see:** a tall figure with glowing eyes among the trees,
     walking away.
3. At the pass.
   - **You should see:** something steps out ahead and comes for the van,
     and fuel starts dripping. Either drive away, or hide: pull over, engine
     off (X), lights off (L), both out, hold E at the back for the tarp, and
     keep out of sight. It loses interest in 20-40 s.

### 7. The coast watchtower

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **7. The coast watchtower**.

**The idea:** sneak past a creature on foot, climb the tower, and stamp
the beach on your map.

1. Get out (or look round, if you're already out).
   - **You should see:** a creature pacing round the base of the tower.
2. Get to the **ramp at the back** without being seen. Crouch behind the
   rocks, the broken wall or the crates. Peek with RMB / LT. There are
   "FREE BOXES" by the road, and E puts one over you.
3. Walk up the ramp to the deck.
   - **You should see:** the sea, the beach, and the Five Roses' dune.
4. Open the map (M) and stamp the beach (the map shows the keys).
   - **You should see:** the objective is done.
5. If it catches you, you wake up somewhere nearby.

## Bessi beach

### 8. The photo

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **8. The photo**. You land on the beach.

**The idea:** only P2 has the photo. Together, find the one spot where it
was taken.

1. Wait a few seconds.
   - **You should see:** P2's phone buzzes. P2 opens it (D-pad Right): the
     lighthouse, a green kiosk with a boat's mast rising out of its roof,
     and Naresh at the edge.
2. **P2:** describe the photo. Both of you use binoculars and tags to point
   at the lighthouse, the memorial, the kiosk and the mast.
3. Walk along the promenade until the lighthouse is right behind the
   memorial's spire **and** the mast comes out of the kiosk roof. Face the
   memorial.
   - **You should see:** "This is it... He stood exactly here."

### 9. The Five Roses

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **9. The Five Roses**. You land on the photo spot: face the memorial and wait a second.

**The idea:** five stone carvings. Open them in the order you travelled.

1. Wait.
   - **You should see:** smoke rolls in from the sea, a rumble, and the
     roses rise on the dune.
2. Walk up. Each rose has a plaque at its foot (windmill, water drop,
   bridge, wave, star). Touch one out of order (E).
   - **You should see:** a boom, and the roses close again.
3. Touch them in order: windmill, water drop, bridge, wave, star.
   - **You should see:** each rose opens. After the star, the fifth bloom
     comes down with Naresh sitting in it. Walk up: "You came! He said you
     would."

### 10. The store shutter

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **10. The store shutter**. You land at the store, Naresh with you.

**The idea:** the shutter only stays up while someone holds it, and the
box inside takes two to carry. So Naresh holds the shutter.

1. Look at the shutter's yellow handle, press V and pick **"Hold the
   shutter"**.
   - **You should see:** it rolls up.
2. Both crouch (Ctrl / B) and go in under it.
   - **You should see:** "Oops!" and it slams down, so you're shut in. A
     few seconds later he opens it again.
3. Both pick up the box (E / X) and carry it out.
   - **You should see:** fresh batteries in both torches, and the objective
     moves on.

### 11. The upturned boat

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **11. The upturned boat**. You land at the boat, Naresh with you.

**The idea:** it takes all three of you to push the boat off the drum.

1. You two hold E / X on the boat from the land side. Look at the boat,
   press V and pick **"Work the boat"**.
   - **You should see:** he goes round to the sea side and pushes the wrong
     way ("Is it moving?").
2. V on the boat again.
   - **You should see:** "Oh! THAT way." With all three of you, it rocks
     and slides off. Under it is a 40 L fuel drum: two of you carry it, and
     it pours into the van like a can. About 6 s later the storm comes.

## The return

### 12. The fishing village (the decoy)

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **12. The fishing village (the decoy)**.

**The idea:** two creatures guard the net shed and follow Naresh. Send him
out to the end of the jetty as bait, grab the fuel, then call him back.

1. Walk halfway to the huts. Don't go in.
   - **You should see:** two creatures pacing by the brown shed.
2. Send Naresh out: binoculars on the far end of the jetty, press V and
   pick **"Go and wait there"**.
   - **You should see:** he walks out, and the creatures slowly follow him.
3. **P1:** go up the fish crates at the side of the shed onto the roof.
   Take the key from the blue boat (E), go back down and unlock the door
   (E).
   - **You should see:** two cans, one heavy and one light. Carry the
     heavy one to the van's rack.
4. **P2:** hide past the shed's corner and watch with the binoculars. When
   a creature gets close to Naresh, binoculars on him, press D-pad Up and
   pick **"Follow me"**.
   - **You should see:** he comes back, picks up the light can on his own
     way and says "Leave the fuel to me!" Let him. The gauge doesn't move.
5. Drive on.
   - **You should see:** the van dies about 200 m on and a creature steps
     out ahead. Take the heavy can off the rack and hold E at the fuel cap
     (driver's side, at the back).

*If he's taken:* he shouts from somewhere high. Walk there, and start
again from step 2.

### 13. The salt pans (red light, green light)

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **13. The salt pans**.

**The idea:** a watcher on a gantry sweeps its gaze, which you can see as
a pale beam. Drive while it looks away, and stop behind a salt heap when
it turns your way.

1. Watch first.
   - **You should see:** it stares along the road a few times, then looks
     out to sea for a few seconds (that's your best moment to go).
2. Both in the van, then cross the flats in short dashes, stopping behind
   the white heaps.
   - **You should see:** behind a heap you're safe. Naresh calls "Stop!"
     (wrong about one time in three). Seen = it comes for the van; drive on
     and it falls behind.

### 14. The swing bridge

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **14. The swing bridge**.

**The idea:** two wheels turn the span, but only while someone holds the
red brake lever off.

1. **P1:** hold E on a wheel.
   - **You should see:** nothing moves, and the sign says BRAKE ON.
2. **P1:** look at the red lever, press V and pick **"Hold the brake"**.
   **Both:** hold a wheel each (E / X).
   - **You should see:** the span swings back.
3. About half way, Naresh lets go to wave at a boat.
   - **You should see:** the span swings back open. Tell him again (V,
     "Hold the brake") and carry on turning.
4. **You should see:** the bolts drop and the barriers go. The yellow
   float on the tide post rises the longer you take.

### 15. The rail tunnel

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **15. The rail tunnel**.

**The idea:** a steel gate blocks the road. Its winch is in a pitch-dark
side passage with a creature in it. P2 sneaks in (and finds the flare gun);
P1 drives through.

1. Headlights (L) and torches on.
   - **You should see:** the gate ahead, and a doorway marked SERVICE
     GALLERY in the wall on the right.
2. **P2:** torch off, crouch (B), go in. Step into the short side passages
   when the creature comes. A quick torch flash reads the "GATE >>" arrows.
3. **P2:** go past the FLOOD GATE WINCH to the next side passage. The red
   case at its end holds the flare gun (X). RB fires it, which scares
   creatures off for 90 s (you have 3 flares).
4. **P2:** back at the winch, hold X. **P1:** drive under the gate. **P2:**
   let go, walk on to the second doorway, and get in.
5. *Optional, the push start:* past the tunnel, on the downhill, engine off
   (X), then Van → **Flatten the battery**. Let the handbrake off (Space),
   and press X once it's rolling.

### 16. The radio mast (the finale)

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **16. The radio mast**. P2 has the flare gun.

**The idea:** start the generator, then point three dishes at three far
blinking red lights. The noise brings creatures.

1. **P2:** hold X on the generator's choke. **P1:** hold E on the pull
   cord.
   - **You should see:** it roars into life, three red lights start
     blinking far away, and the screen on the desk lights up.
2. **P2:** stand guard and fire a flare (RB) at any creature that gets
   close.
3. *Optional:* climb the mast and tag the lights (T).
4. **P1:** hold E on the "dish 1" crank. Let go when its red light is in
   the middle of the screen.
   - **You should see:** its lamp turns green.
5. **Dish 2 is Naresh's:** V on the crank, **"Hold"**. He turns it the
   wrong way, so give him V → "Let go of it", then V → "Hold" again. When
   it's centred, V on **him** → "Wait here".
6. **P1:** dish 3, like dish 1.
   - **You should see:** "The mast hums... the clouds break over the West
     Road."
