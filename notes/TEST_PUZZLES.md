# The puzzles: your play sheet

All 16 puzzles in journey order, so you can play each one and we can
discuss what to change, one at a time (your plan, 2026-10-05). Each one
has: how to get there with F1, the idea, the steps, and what you should
see. When you've played one, tell me its number and what you'd change.

Two players: P1 on keyboard + mouse, P2 on the controller, split screen.

**Every puzzle starts the same way:** start `Play.bat` and pick **New
game**. Press **F1**, go to the **Puzzles** tab (arrow keys), pick the
puzzle and press **Enter**, then press **F1** (or Esc) to close the menu.
This puts the puzzle back to how you first find it (also after you've done
it), sets the story to its step and puts you both there. From Bessi on it
gives you the binoculars and brings Naresh along.

**Testing alone (one keyboard, no controller; 2026-10-07):** **TAB**
moves the keyboard to the other player. The player you leave **keeps
holding** whatever they held at that moment (E on a lever or a crank, W
up a ladder): their screen says "HOLDING E", and yours says "P1 is holding
E". TAB back and let go to release them. So: P1 holds the lever (hold E),
press TAB, let go of E, play P2. Let go of W before you press TAB, or the
player you leave keeps walking. F2 shows both views at once. Switch it off
in F1 → Puzzles (the first row).

**Controls**

| | P1 | P2 (pad) |
|---|---|---|
| Use / hold | E | X |
| Tag | T | RT |
| Binoculars, peek | hold RMB | hold LT |
| Crouch | Ctrl | B |

## The way out

### 1. The water works (reworked 2026-10-05: your changes)

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **1. The water works**. (It puts P2's
coolant jug, empty, on the van's rack, as after the opening, and the van's
hose split.)

**The idea:** the pumper works inside the brick pump house and can't see
the tanks. The valve player is out in the tank yard and can't see the
pressure. You talk the whole time: every mistake costs you, often your
partner.

1. **The pump house door is padlocked** (its door faces the tanks).
   **Naresh's note** is pinned on it (E reads it): the key's up the water
   tower. His notes tell you how everything here works: the door, the pump,
   valve B, the tap, the standpipe. They collect in the van's journal
   (second page: A / D, pad D-Left).
   - *Exactly how:* ladder on the tower's road side: E, hold W. On the
     catwalk walk a quarter of the way round, towards the yard; the key is
     on the tank wall: E. Back to the ladder, look at it, E, hold S. At the
     door: E.
   - **You should see:** from the catwalk, the whole yard and its pipes
     from above (handy for the valve player). The door swings open.
2. **Pumper:** inside, at the red pump: **press E once each time the lever
   comes back up** (one stroke at a time). Keep the needle in the green.
   - **You should see:** each stroke pushes the needle up a little. Press
     too fast (before the lever is back up) and it jumps; too high and the
     relief valve bangs, and the pump stalls a few seconds. Pause a beat
     now and then to hold it in the green.
3. **Try the mistake once:** pump before the valves are set, with the
   valve player standing in the yard.
   - **You should see:** after 2-3 strokes the grey **SLUDGE** tank is
     full. It gurgles, groans for 2 s, then its lid blows and sludge rains
     over the whole yard. The valve player is covered: brown, dripping,
     squelching, everything in slow motion (walking, looking, jumping,
     turning valves) for ~25 s. The pumper inside just hears a boom; the
     needle drops and the lever goes slack for 4 s.
4. **Rinse off (optional):** the standpipe in the yard, by the pump house:
   hold E / X under it for about 3 s.
5. **Valve player:** follow the pipes and turn valves A and B (E / X) so
   the water goes to the **blue** tank, **only while the pumper isn't
   pumping**.
   - **You should see:** turned under pressure, a "Glug": a fifth of the
     blue tank surges back into the grey one (which then fills even
     faster). Shout "stop!" first, then turn.
6. **Pump on.** Valve B slips twice ("WORN SEAT").
   - **You should see:** a CLANK, B's pointer swings, a gulp of blue into
     grey, and the needle falls. Pumper stops, valve player turns B back,
     pumper carries on.
7. **Fill P2's coolant jug at the tap.** When the blue tank is full, take
   the jug off the van's rack (back of the van, right-hand slot), hold it
   to the **brass tap** at the blue tank's foot and hold E / X (5 L in 5 s).
   Carry it to the van's grille and hold E / X to pour.
   - **You should see:** the hissing stops (the hose is sealed). The turbine
     started when the tank filled; the lamps light along the road north.
   - **No jug?** The old oil can on the pump house bench holds 4 L but leaks
     a litre a minute: hurry.
8. **The Memory Fragment** is on the pump house bench, by Naresh's mug.

*If stuck:* A points on to valve B, and B points to the blue tank.

### 2. The windmill (reworked 2026-10-07: your changes)

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **2. The windmill**. You start in the
van on the lane, 30 m short of the junction gate.

**The idea:** the junction gate has no power, and the old windmill that
fed it has stopped. One of you holds the starter lever the whole time;
the other runs round the legs, head-butting each one back as it slides.
Too slow, and the windmill falls on you.

1. **Drive up to the gate.** It's a red-and-white barrier across the road
   ("JUNCTION CLOSED / NO POWER"), with fences and boulders either side.
   The windmill stands off to the left.
   - **You should see:** the van can't get past. Both of you get out.
2. **Over the stile** (a gap with a step in the fence beside the road,
   marked FOOTPATH) into the windmill's meadow. At the **starter lever** (a
   red handle on a post between the road and the windmill), read
   **Naresh's note** on the post (E / X). It tells you what to do.
3. **Lever player:** hold E / X on the lever, and **keep holding** (you can
   look round while you hold).
   - **You should see:** the fan creeps round, then faster and faster, with
     a creak that quickens and a rising whoosh. Let go and it runs down
     again (twice as fast as it wound up).
4. **The other player:** stand under the windmill. Four times as it speeds
   up, one leg screeches (the sound comes from that leg) and its foot
   **slides out** off its cracked stone footing: dust, its light flashes
   red, the whole windmill leans that way. Run to it, face the leg and
   **head-butt it: G / RB**.
   - **You should see:** your head rears back, lunges, stars and "CLANG!";
     the foot jumps back with dust and a white mark appears on the footing
     (up to 3). Back in place: the bolt plate slams down, the light turns
     green and stays green. A leg can slip again once in a while. The lever
     player has the better view (and the power dial on the switch house):
     they shout (or tag it, T / RT) which leg is going.
5. **Try the mistake once:** let a leg slide for 8 s.
   - **You should see:** a groan, the windmill leans further, and 2 s later
     it falls **on whoever is standing next to it** (or, if nobody is, on
     the one at the lever). Under it: a crash, dust, the
     screen goes white. A few seconds later it stands again and you're
     both by the gate, ready to start over (your partner gets "you ok? I
     think I'm flat").
6. **Keep holding until it catches the wind** (about 45 s of holding).
   - **You should see:** a deeper hum; the switch house's window lights
     up and a spark runs along the wire, pole to pole, to the gate (the
     barrier swings up), then on, lighting the street lamps one by one.
     The lever player can let go: the wind keeps it turning.
7. **The ladder:** a few seconds later the shaking shakes the rolled-up
   rope ladder loose; it clatters down the back of the windmill. Climb it
   (E / X, hold W / stick up): 24 m, about 10 s.
   - **You should see:** from the walkway round the head, the fan
     thundering past and the whole valley: "It all goes onto the map".
8. **The chest** is right in front of you as you step off the ladder:
   E / X opens it, again to take the **napin**. Naresh's note is on the
   wall just above it.
   - **You should see:** whoever has the map can now stick the napin in it
     (M, then click / A); the van's nav points at it.
9. **Down:** look at the top of the ladder, E / X, hold S / stick down.

**The fun mechanics (try them any time from now):**
- **Head-butt** (G / RB, empty hands): your partner staggers back; the van
  rocks; a can flies. Not creatures.
- **The map:** only one of you has it (P2 from the opening). The other's M
  says who has it. Walk up to them, look at them and press E / X to
  **take it off them**, even while they're reading it, and run. Works
  between the van's seats too.
- **Push out of the van:** now the barn maze's reward (#3): after its chest,
  seated, **G** (pad: **hold RB** half a second) shoves the other one out of
  their door, at any speed.

### 3. The barn maze (optional, Valley Road; reworked 2026-10-08, rounds 2 and 3 2026-10-12/13)

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **3. The barn maze**. You start out of
the van, parked in the yard in front of the red barn.

**The idea:** a big hedge maze behind the barn (63 m across, hedges 3 m
high). One of you guides from the top of the barn's silo, the other walks
in carrying a flag. Halfway it snows so hard the guide can't see anything:
they guide from what they remembered.

1. **Round the barn** (past the silo) to the way in: a painted arch,
   "HEDGE MAZE". **Naresh's note** is on the left post (E / X).
2. **Guide:** climb the **cage ladder on the silo** (E / X, hold W / stick
   up; 13 m). Read his **note on the rail** and look at his **sketch**
   pinned beside it: half the maze, some landmarks, the rest unfinished.
   - **You should see:** the near half of the maze like a map; the far
     half only as hedge tops. Dark **square** patches of straw on the
     paths are **pits** (the round straw patches are just straw).
3. **Walker:** take a **flag** from the rack by the arch (E / X). Its red
   rag sticks up above the hedges, so the guide can follow you. Go in.
4. **Guide:** talk the walker through (tags, T / RT, work too). The
   landmarks: tall ones you both see (a weathervane rooster, a carousel
   horse, a dead tree full of crows, a hay elevator), low ones only the
   walker sees (a plank over a ditch, a broken chair, a well, a bathtub of
   flowers, a red phone box, a stack of tyres) and twins (two chairs,
   one broken; two scarecrows, one with a lantern). Painted arrows on the
   ground: only the guide can read them, and two of them lie.
5. **Try the mistake once:** step on a dark square.
   - **You should see:** a crack, the boards drop, black; you climb out of
     a **drain by the front gate**, straw all over your view. Back to the
     start.
6. **Try a wrong turn,** then walk back out of it.
   - **You should see:** at a corner, something **pops out of the hedge**
     at you (a scarecrow, a crow, a cow's head, a mannequin, a lantern),
     with a sting the first time. It never happens on the way in.
7. **The courtyard in the middle** (a farm bell on a frame):
   - **You should see:** the sky darkens, the wind howls, and within ~10 s
     it **snows hard**: the walker sees about 10 m, the guide nothing but
     white. Snow settles on the hedge tops and covers the ground (and the
     pit squares). Your footprints show and fill in after half a minute.
     A text from Naresh: "it's SNOWING".
   - **Ring the bell** (E / X): the guide hears where you are.
8. **The back half, from memory:** the guide calls it ("after the plank,
   left; if you see the broken chair you're right"). Mind the pit on the
   way: it's still there under the snow.
8a. **Round 3:**
   - **Flags:** the rack holds two. Take both: one up on its pole, one spare
     on your back. Nothing takes them, except a **hidden gap**: a low hole
     at the foot of a hedge, half behind brambles, a shortcut you crouch
     through (Ctrl / B). The pole snags and stays stuck in the brambles; the
     spare goes up. Four **old marker flags** stand round the maze: E / X
     takes one (two at most).
   - **The snow** is a blizzard for the walker; from the silo you still see
     the maze and the flag through it.
   - **The plan:** at the board on the silo rail, E / X opens it big. Where
     the walker has been shows faint; **hold E / X** (3 s) to draw it in
     (hedges, doors, landmarks by name). **Click / A** pencils a cross,
     right-click / X rubs it out, M / D-Down puts it away. It doesn't show
     where the walker is: the flag does that.
   - **Frights** only come when the walker goes round in circles (back over
     ground they've walked): up to three, at random. Getting somewhere new,
     closer to the chest, ends it.
8b. **Round 2, the snow half (past the courtyard):**
   - **The guide's van run:** up top it's all white now. Naresh's note on
     the rail says: get the van round the track to the back gate and honk.
     Climb down, drive the dirt track round the maze's left side (slow, in
     the snow), park by the EXIT board and the windsock, honk (Q / L3).
   - **Doors** in the hedges, all alike: three are the way on (the first
     two swing shut behind you and won't open from that side), one leads
     round a loop back to itself, four open onto a brick wall.
   - **The music box:** run, rush the corners, spin round, and it gets
     louder; stop and look and it fades. Standing still, it's louder down a
     wrong turn.
   - **Frights, on the way in too:** sprint round a corner or whip round
     and a figure is right there for a moment (caught running, you stumble
     and drop the flag: pick it up again). Something on the ground (a doll,
     a red glove, a shoe, a music box): look at it, turn back, and something
     is standing there. Sounds from beyond the hedge: a crow, a bucket, three
     knocks, a door creaking. A scarecrow down the corridor comes a bit
     nearer every time you look away. Small footprints run ahead in the
     snow, and twice they go the wrong way.
9. **The feed chest** in the far corner (E / X): **a spare tyre, a fuel
   can (10 L) and a coolant jug**, and **Naresh's note in the lid: the
   push**. The chest's bay is marked by a red lantern and pennant on a tall
   pole; the exit by a windsock, an EXIT board and a lamp that lights when
   the snow comes.
   - **You should see:** the snow stops and **it clears**: sun on the white
     hedges for about 3 minutes; then the clouds close in again.
10. **The back gate** beside the chest opens from inside (E / X). Carry
    the things to the van. The **spare** goes on the van's empty rear
    mount: hold E / X at the back of the van.
11. **The push** (learned now): in the van, seated, **G** (pad: hold RB)
    shoves the other one out, even driving.

**The weather from now on (your plan):** clouds from the very start, far
thunder now and then; from the windmill a light snowfall while you drive;
after the maze the world slowly turns white (a dusting by Last Fuel, white
fields by the bridge; the road stays dark); over the ghat it stops and
melts towards the coast and the storm. Clear sun only on the drive home.

### 4. The lookout boards (optional, Ridge Track; reworked 2026-10-14)

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **4. The lookout boards**.

**The idea:** the Ridge Patrol's box has four picture dials. The code is
painted on landmarks out in the valley. One of you reads them from the
deck with the binoculars. The other reads the patrol's riddles at the box
and turns the dials.

1. **Top player:** walk up the ramp. Take the binoculars off the bench.
   Hold RMB / LT to look. Scroll the mouse wheel (pad: RB / LB) to zoom,
   from 2x to 8x.
   - **You should see:** the zoom under the eyepieces ("4x"). At 6x and
     above the view sways. Crouch, or stand at the rail, and it steadies.
2. **Top player:** look round the valley. Find the pictures: a dark
   square with a white shape, on the windmill, the switch house by it,
   the red barn, the silo, the Last Fuel sign and the far water tower.
   - **You should see:** the barn and silo sit in a grey snow shower. It
     thins every 20 seconds or so; wait for it. The water tower is far:
     8x and a steady view.
   - The table on the deck points to the places. A piece is broken off.
3. **Bottom player:** at the green box, read the sheet on its left end
   (E / X). Read Naresh's note on the back.
   - **You should see:** four riddles. The fourth is "on the patrol sign,
     up the slope".
4. **Bottom player:** the patrol sign is covered in old snow, too high to
   reach. Make a snowball from the old snow under the deck (E / X). Throw
   it at the sign (G / LMB, pad RB). Each hit knocks a lump off.
   - **You should see:** the riddle appears when the last lump falls.
5. **Both:** work out the riddles together. Turn the dials (E / X), then
   pull the handle on the right-hand end.
   - **You should see:** wrong, a clunk and the dials spin round on their
     own (start again). Right, the lid springs up: a full can of fuel.
6. **For fun:** throw snowballs at each other, at Naresh, at the van.
   - **You should see:** "SPLAT!" on your partner; a white splat on the
     van that melts away. A snowball melts in your hands after 4 minutes.

**Stuck?** Hold H: the hint gets clearer the longer you're there
(read out loud; read every shape; snowballs). **The answer:** 1 the
windmill, 2 the silo, 3 the shape found twice (on the barn and on Last
Fuel), 4 the water tower at the coolant works.

### 5. The lift bridge

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **5. The lift bridge**. The water works counts as done, so the power is on.

**The idea:** the operator in the hut can only see the machinery through a
safety mirror. The partner across the road clears the jammed gear, then
rides the counterweight down.

1. **Operator:** walk up the little ramp into the hut. Hold E on LOWER.
   - **You should see:** the motor strains and the leaf shudders. A message
     says it's jammed. The mirror outside the window shows the machinery
     house.
2. **Partner:** walk out on the deck and into the machinery house across
   the road. Hold X on the wedge in the gear.
   - **You should see:** "Wedged tight... back it off (RAISE)".
3. **Operator:** hold RAISE while the partner holds X on the wedge.
   - **You should see:** after about 2.5 s the wedge drops into the river.
4. **Operator:** hold LOWER.
   - **You should see:** below about 30°, the cut-out trips and the leaf
     goes back up to 45°.
5. **Operator:** watch the mirror and let go of LOWER when the
   counterweight in the pit comes up level with the floor. **Partner:**
   step onto it. **Operator:** LOWER again.
   - **You should see:** the partner rides up, the leaf comes down and
     locks, and the barriers go. You can drive across.

   - **You should see:** two creatures pacing by the brown shed.

     "Hold the brake") and carry on turning.
4. **You should see:** the bolts drop and the barriers go. The yellow
   float on the tide post rises the longer you take.

### 15. The rail tunnel

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **15. The rail tunnel**.

**The idea:** a steel gate blocks the road. Its winch is in a pitch-dark
side passage with a creature in it. P2 sneaks in (and finds the flare gun);
P1 drives through.

1. Headlights (L) and torches on.
   - **You should see:** the gate ahead, and a doorway marked SERVICE
     GALLERY in the wall on the right.
2. **P2:** torch off, crouch (B), go in. Step into the short side passages
   when the creature comes. A quick torch flash reads the "GATE >>" arrows.
3. **P2:** go past the FLOOD GATE WINCH to the next side passage. The red
   case at its end holds the flare gun (X). RB fires it, which scares
   creatures off for 90 s (you have 3 flares).
4. **P2:** back at the winch, hold X. **P1:** drive under the gate. **P2:**
   let go, walk on to the second doorway, and get in.
5. *Optional, the push start:* past the tunnel, on the downhill, engine off
   (X), then Van → **Flatten the battery**. Let the handbrake off (Space),
   and press X once it's rolling.

### 16. The radio mast (the finale)

**Getting there:** F1 → Puzzles → **16. The radio mast**. P2 has the flare gun.

**The idea:** start the generator, then point three dishes at three far
blinking red lights. The noise brings creatures.

1. **P2:** hold X on the generator's choke. **P1:** hold E on the pull
   cord.
   - **You should see:** it roars into life, three red lights start
     blinking far away, and the screen on the desk lights up.
2. **P2:** stand guard and fire a flare (RB) at any creature that gets
   close.
3. *Optional:* climb the mast and tag the lights (T).
4. **P1:** hold E on the "dish 1" crank. Let go when its red light is in
   the middle of the screen.
   - **You should see:** its lamp turns green.
5. **Dish 2 is Naresh's:** V on the crank, **"Hold"**. He turns it the
   wrong way, so give him V → "Let go of it", then V → "Hold" again. When
   it's centred, V on **him** → "Wait here".
6. **P1:** dish 3, like dish 1.
   - **You should see:** "The mast hums... the clouds break over the West
     Road."
