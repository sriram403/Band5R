# Milestone D: your test checklist

Two players (P1 on keyboard + mouse, P2 on the controller), split screen.
Start `Play.bat`, **New game**. Play the opening as before, or skip it:
**F1** (developer menu) -> "Skip to the next objective" **once** (it skips the
whole opening; the objective becomes *"Drive up the lane to the windmill"*),
then "Teleport both players to: < windmill >" (left / right picks the place)
and "Bring the van here". Press F1 to close. The objective changes to *"The
windmill is jammed..."* once the van is there.

For each step: **what to do**, then **what should happen**. Note anything that
feels wrong, slow, confusing or ugly, with the step number.

Controls you'll need: use E / pad X · tag T / pad RT · binoculars or peek hold
RMB / pad LT · crouch Ctrl / pad B · map M / pad D-Down · swing the nav N /
pad A · engine X · lights L · handbrake Space / pad B · horn Q · hint hold H /
pad R3 · F1 developer menu (teleport, bring the van, skip).

## 1. Windmill (J1)
1. Drive up to the windmill. **Expect:** an old text from Naresh; the
   objective says the windmill is jammed.
2. One player: the ladder at the **back** of the tower. E, hold W to climb.
   **Expect:** you step off onto a platform under the blades.
3. Climber: look at the blade with the rope round it and tag it (T / RT).
   **Expect:** a pin on both screens, "the snagged blade"; it follows the blade.
4. Other player: the red **brake lever** out front. E lets it off. **Expect:**
   the blades creep round, creaking.
5. Put the brake back on when the tagged blade reaches the bottom, by the
   platform. **Expect:** it stops; the climber can "Hold to cut the rope".
   (Too early or late: "out of reach", try again.)
6. Climber holds E to cut. Brake off. **Expect:** the blades spin up; a few
   seconds later the miller's box at the foot springs open.
7. Take the map from the box, open your map (M). **Expect:** both roads to
   Last Fuel drawn in, with the lake, barn, lookout and wreck.
8. Climb down (look at the ladder's top from the platform, E, hold S).

*Tell me:* was the brake timing too easy / too hard? Was it clear what to do?

## 2a. Valley road: the barn maze (optional)
1. Stop at the red barn. On its west end: a hedge maze and a **ladder** up to
   a loft balcony.
2. One climbs to the balcony, the other walks into the maze.
3. The loft player guides (tags on the turns). **Expect:** from the loft you
   can see the whole maze; inside you only see hedges.
4. Walk into the middle. **Expect:** hay dust blows over the middle; the loft
   can't see in; the walker describes the gate / scarecrow / drum / cart.
5. At the far end, open the feed chest. **Expect:** a coolant jug and a crate.

*Tell me:* fun? Too long (it's 33 cells), too short? Did the dust help or annoy?

## 2b. Ridge track: the lookout code (optional, do it on a second run or reload)
1. At the Pine Ridge lookout: walk up the ramp. **Expect:** you step
   properly onto the deck now (the old lip is gone). Take the binoculars.
2. Look out across the valley: two small black boards, "1" and "2".
   **Expect:** by eye the pictures are just pale dots; hold RMB / LT and you
   can read them (circle, square, triangle, cross, ring, diamond).
3. The other player at the green supply box by the ramp's foot: four dials,
   numbered 1-4 left to right. E / X turns one.
4. Call the pictures down (board 1 = dials 1, 2; board 2 = dials 3, 4).
   **Expect:** "CLACK", the box opens, a full fuel can inside.

*Tell me:* readable through the binoculars? Too easy?

## 3. Last Fuel and the water works
1. At Last Fuel, read the **note on the kiosk door**. **Expect:** the power's
   been off since the turbine stopped; the bridge runs off the same line.
2. Refuel from the cans as before, drive to the water works, do the valves
   and the pump as before.
3. When the blue tank fills. **Expect:** a message; the **turbine** by the
   river starts turning (the big wheel on its yard side); lamps come on one
   after another along the poles beside the road, north to the bridge.

*Tell me:* could you see the lamps come on? Did it make you want to go north?

## 4. The lift bridge
1. Drive to the bridge. **Expect:** the middle is a steel-edged leaf stuck up,
   barriers "BRIDGE UP"; a hut "BRIDGE CONTROL" beside the road with its lamp on.
2. Operator: into the hut (up the little ramp). Panel with **RAISE** and
   **LOWER**; a yellow-framed **safety mirror** outside the window.
3. Hold E on LOWER. **Expect:** the motor strains, the leaf shudders, a message
   about a jam; the mirror shows the machinery house.
4. Partner: walk out on the near deck, into the **machinery house** across the
   road (door from the deck). Find the wedge in the gear. Hold E on it.
   **Expect:** "Wedged tight... back it off (RAISE)".
5. Operator holds RAISE while the partner holds E on the wedge. **Expect:**
   after ~2.5 s it drops into the river.
6. Operator holds LOWER. **Expect:** below ~30 deg the cut-out trips and the
   leaf goes back up to 45, with a message about the counterweight.
7. Watch the mirror (or the panel's angle) and let go of LOWER when the
   counterweight in the pit comes up **level with the floor** (~35-40 deg).
   Partner steps onto it.
8. LOWER again. **Expect:** the partner rides up on the counterweight; the
   leaf comes down and locks; barriers vanish. Partner jumps off.
9. Drive across.

*Tell me:* was the mirror useful and readable in split screen? Did the
counterweight step make sense, or did it feel like a guess?

## 5. The ghat hairpins
1. Drive east from J3 up the hills. **Expect:** fog comes down on the hairpins.
2. Passenger swings the nav (N / pad A). **Expect:** pace notes, e.g.
   "70 LEFT HAIRPIN / DON'T CUT / 170 LEFT 3"; the driver's view of the nav
   is blank. Call them to the driver.
3. On the second hairpin, look out to the side. **Expect:** a tall figure with
   glowing eyes between the trees, walking away (it does nothing).
4. At the pass, with both in the van. **Expect:** something tall steps out
   ahead and comes for the van; a message; after ~2 s dripping (fuel leak).
5. Try hiding: pull over, engine off (X), lights off (L), both out, hold E at
   the back of the van for 4 s (tarp). Keep out of its sight. **Expect:** it
   circles, loses interest in 20-40 s and walks off; "It's gone".
   (Or just drive away: that ends it too.)

*Tell me:* is the fog too thick / too thin? Pace notes readable and useful?
Did you notice the glimpse? Was the attack scary / clear / too long?

## 6. The coast watchtower
1. Down Beach Road towards the sea; stop by the tall watchtower. Get out.
   **Expect:** a message: something is pacing round its base.
2. Get to the **ramp at the back** without being seen: rocks, a broken wall,
   crates on the way; "FREE BOXES" by the road. Crouch behind a rock and peek
   (hold RMB / LT) to watch it.
3. Up the ramp (16 m). **Expect:** first view of the sea, the beach, the Five
   Roses on the skyline.
4. Open the map, stamp the beach. **Expect:** objective done; the van's nav
   points there. The objective then says milestone E comes next.
5. If it catches you: you wake somewhere nearby (as in the stealth gym).

*Tell me:* was sneaking up fair? Too easy? Did the boxes get used?

## 7. Saving
1. At any point after step 4, save at the van's journal (J), quit, load.
   **Expect:** windmill free, map kept, lamps on, bridge down, puzzles solved
   stay solved.

## 8. General
- Sounds: turbine, bridge motor hum, machinery, creaks (I can't listen).
- Anything that looks wrong: floating, clipping, can't walk somewhere, text
  hard to read in split screen.
- The look of the new block-outs (approve now, or for the polish stage):
  windmill platform, poles and lamps, bridge hut, machinery house, lift leaf,
  safety mirror, watchtower cover, hay maze, code boards; still open: the
  tarp and the cardboard box.
