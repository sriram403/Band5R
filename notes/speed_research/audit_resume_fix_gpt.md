## Each design piece

- **A1 bodies — KEEP.** SG:163-166 unfreezes/zeroes the van; SG:253 zeroes item velocity; T:58-60 measured real differences. Restore original velocity/freeze/sleep, not zero; later unfreezing matters. Cost: one keyed body record each. Break it: a stale velocity is also read directly by GH:729, so “not moving” cannot mean “value irrelevant”. Observe item freeze/freeze_mode and collision state too (GO:52-55 currently omits them).
- **A2 plain vars — SIMPLIFY to explicit restore contracts plus the automatic scalar snapshot.** Restoring engine_on directly avoids VA:1608/1638 click/temperature side effects. But a skip list discovered by layer 2 cannot expose fields GO never reads: raw crouching=true can leave a standing capsule, since PR:483-487 changes it only on a boolean transition. Cost: adapters/observations for these coupled fields, not blanket exclusions.
- **A3 hearing — KEEP with an atomic age restore.** HE:30 prunes on emit, HE:37-41 consumes by serial, CR:450 compares event time against _item_ms. Save ordered events, IDs, radius, positions, next ID and listener cursors together. Cost: queue copy plus one common clock offset. Break it: rebasing the queue without _item_ms changes bounce suppression; rebasing just once lets the verdict wait expire events.
- **A4 clocks — KEEP, correct the units/sentinels.** GI:13-15 mixes ms, seconds, and unset values; D:47-48's single ms formula cannot be applied to _cap_t in seconds (VA:1645-1647). Preserve unset semantics and signed ages; normalise by age, not raw stamps. Cost: three typed rules plus hearing; test each side of its expiry boundary.
- **A4 boundary RNG — KEEP only as a scoped, conditional solution.** D:49-51 reseeds fresh and resume; that is a changed test protocol, not saved old RNG state. Use a stored integer seed and protocol version; seed both at the identical last point before actions. Cost: tiny reseed, substantial caller/scheduling audit. Break it: render-driven PR:420 consumes global random numbers; SX:52/70 and SF:164 also draw. Equal seeds do not imply equal draw order.
- **B pause/settle — KEEP the paused wait; CUT the “move observation before the hold” escape.** LC:660-672 currently observes then runs a second plus client wait. D:58-59 would let the gate approve an earlier state that deferred work changes before input. Require immediate observation AND a final pre-input invariant check; abort on a changed gameplay field. Cost: two observations and a small bench. See clocks/children below.
- **B1.1 frozen van — KEEP conditional on a complete, validated body record.** WG:161-164 currently refuses stale frozen velocity; SG:163 is the actual unfreeze bug. Preserve exact L2 velocity/freeze/sleep checks. Cost: one predicate. False SAME if the record's presence is treated as proof of correct contents (D:62-63).
- **B1.2 hearing/listener/wall refusals — SIMPLIFY to versioned adapter admission.** Do not clear all listener refusals from two key names (D:64-66): Problem children are not restored by these keys (LC:547-552). Compare ordered IDs/radius/positions/cursors and age relationships. A free 50 ms allowance can cross HE:21/30's 2000 ms prune boundary. Cost: validated schema and edge controls, not a wider timing tolerance.
- **B1.3 RNG refusal — KEEP only after caller and draw-order proof.** Presence of rng_seed is insufficient (D:67; PR:420 versus CR:671). Require supported protocol/profile and refuse unhandled consumers. Cost: protocol check plus an exercised sniff control; P16 alone does not exercise sniff randomness (GH:722-725, CR:668-675).
- **B1.4 held carryable exemption — CUT as written.** CA:6-10 explicitly says the held item is a real colliding body, CA:128-133 can drop it when snagged, CA:144/155 applies velocities. Pick-up does not rebuild contact caches. Cost of retaining refusal: P16 remains ineligible until a held-body adapter/bench proves this exact use. Add item velocity/angular/contact observations and a snag/contact planted pair before a narrow exemption.
- **B1.5 engine-on — KEEP conditional on engine adapter proof, not live_vars alone.** VA:1180 updates a separate audio object; GO:37-38 only observes VanAttack script vars, not engine children. Cost: engine state/audio/visual rebuild plus P2 evidence. If proof row 6 is omitted, engine-on remains unqualified/refused.
- **B1.6 far sleeping tolerance — CUT from v1.** D:70-73 chooses bounds after seeing results; 10 m distance now says nothing about later driving/pickup. A 0.01 m change can cross a nearby interaction/contact threshold even if a different check has a 0.15 m margin. Cost saved: new gate policy and controls. Keep exact L2; investigate the restore if it fails (GI:3, WG:226-235).
- **B1.7 repair — KEEP only for action-only walk repairs with unchanged game, helpers, setup and prefix.** WG:392-405 deliberately checks code and producer identity. Two old SAME runs do not qualify a changed game/helper. Bind REPAIR to the exact qualified point and current restore/gate protocol; preserve all other checks. Never promote REPAIR to SAME or use it as a qualification receipt. Cost: a separate receipt/admission path in both PY:274-323 and WG:438-471.

## Hidden state and failure modes

- **Pause is not a stopped clock.** Godot pauses physics servers, but Always nodes and signals can continue: [official pause rules](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/scripting/pausing_games.html). PT:64 is Always; PT:456 creates an always-running timer. Godot 4.5 SceneTree emits physics_frame without a pause guard ([scene_tree.cpp:584-604](https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4.5/scene/main/scene_tree.cpp#L584)).
- **I think Engine.get_physics_frames() and thus PT.gms() continue during the hold on this binary; that needs a tiny builder bench, not a claim that physics moved.** PT:271-285 derives game time/deadlines from that counter; LC:564 samples at +3000 ms; PR:946/981 stores/tests absolute _climb_since. Snapshot/rebase frame ages or refuse active climbing. Start trajectory/deadline clocks only after release; report paused wall time separately. Do not rewrite the global clock without auditing all its readers.
- **Resume execution phase also matters.** LC:756 starts the sampler immediately before actions; a fresh boundary may be reached after a timer/physics await, while D:55 proposes resuming after drawn frames. I think different first-tick order can change collisions/RNG. Bench both in the same callback phase; do not call “same tick” proof by itself. PT:472-475 shows physics_frame awaits still resume coroutines.
- **Hearing must not age through the hold.** HE:37 does not itself expire events, but the next HE:30 emit prunes them. Freeze logical event ages from point capture through final release, use one offset for queue and _item_ms, and verify ages again after a deliberately delayed verdict. D:56-57 only works if its second rebase includes the queue, not just GI wallstamp patterns.
- **Player body state is missing.** SG:274 saves/restores position only, SG:281 zeroes player velocity; GO:42 compares a full transform/43 velocity. A2's plain script snapshot cannot save inherited CharacterBody velocity/transform (LC:489). Restore full transform and velocity explicitly. Rebuild capsule height/offset, collider disabled, seat/vehicle refs and collision exceptions together (PR:483-487,1896-1927). Observe them independently; matching crouching alone can be a false SAME.
- **Carryable relationships must be rebuilt, not raw-set.** LC:469-497 skips arrays containing Object refs such as CA.holders. CA:81-98 installs/removes collision exceptions; CA:208-237 changes parent, freeze_mode, process_mode and masks for stow. Pick-up plus bodies/vars does not independently verify all of these. Add parent/holders/exceptions/masks/process/freeze-mode observations and required fields; restore in dependency order.
- **Problem's children are a definite omission.** LC:547-552 restores only the Creature's plain vars and root transform. CR:655-666 sets rig/head transforms, including head interpolation using its old child angles; CR:644-647 sets emission and hum. NL:21-26 has gain/filter/phase state. These are observed by GO:173-196 but never restored; new CR:143-208 children start at defaults. Rebuild deterministic visuals without advancing gameplay or explicitly restore children. Do not call _show(delta>0): CR:669-673 advances sniff time and can emit sound/RNG.
- **Timers/audio need an explicit scope decision.** GO:192-193 observes Timer running/time_left, but LC has no timer restore. I found no Timer construction in the inspected Creature build (CR:143-208); if present at an admitted boundary, restore it or refuse. GO:188/191 omits audio playback cursor/stream_paused; NL:32/43 has a 0.3 s generator buffer. SX:67-76 creates world-root one-shots outside Problem, and SX:90's UI player runs Always. Match meaningful sound/envelope/cue state or explicitly limit SAME to gameplay and keep recording/sound acceptance separate; identical PCM is not promised.
- **Global RNG is shared with cosmetic work.** PR:420 draws per rendered frame while shaking, not per physics tick; SX:58 can skip all sound draws when muted. SF:145-164 conditionally draws on weather timers not covered by A2. Require the relevant consumers inactive or deterministically restored/scheduled. I think a test-only isolated gameplay random stream is more robust if this audit cannot close; that would need a revised design, not a silent game-code edit.
- **Input/internal physics state remains outside script equality.** ID:249/254 has latched/mouse state and ID:263-266 reads _held/_prev; GO:74-88 checks physical held inputs only. Refuse or clear/prove idle device state symmetrically. Character floor/contact caches and vehicle wheels are also not represented by root fields (CR:272-273; VA:1444-1454). Admit only supported boundaries and keep bounded trajectory evidence, never claim full engine-state identity (D:103-104).

**Is it enough?** After the changes, rows 1-4 qualify only this point/profile/protocol with the same next step; they cannot prove all restore boundaries (D:107). P16 is phase leave, where CR:668's crouch/sniff condition need not run; add a small exercised RNG bench rather than saying P16 proves A4. Engine-on and held-contact support each need their own evidence before their refusals are lifted.
**Fair perturbations?** X1 and X2 are fair paired tests only if clean counterparts pass and each negative fails at its intended layer/field; unrelated failures are inconclusive (T:66). Also plant wrong capsule/child/adapter contents offline or on the short bench; X1/X2 alone cannot catch those omissions (GO:39-55, LC:547-552).
**Repair safety?** An action-only repair is a useful, explicitly weaker admission. Require two unique clean receipts tied to point bytes, producer identity/profile, seed/restore version and current gate/inventory; rerun qualification after changes to those. Producer hashes must include action-sending client/helper code: T:124-126 records that tools/live.py is currently outside enforced test identity. A game-code bug fix affecting the prefix still needs fresh production (WG:392-403).
**Honest saving?** D:96 adds F's prefix to repair suffix: a synthetic estimate, not a measured fresh fixed walk. Label it so, including F boot/setup once and suffix from the boundary (exclude repair boot/restore/wait from the synthetic suffix), or run one actual fresh fixed walk. CPU/profile/recording mode must match; quote launch-to-DONE wall time and simulated time separately (PT:279-294).
**Budget:** D:89-94 sums to ~11.7 min mandatory, ~16.7 with P2; these are estimates, not observed lifetimes. A real fresh fixed baseline is roughly another 7.7 min by D:83/93, giving ~19.4 without P2, ~24.4 with it, before retries/bench/compile. My pick: keep the synthetic label, reserve the ~3.3 min remainder for a short watched bench, and defer engine admission if necessary. Never omit costs of failed/aborted launches.

## Required numbered changes

1. Define an atomic paused restore/release with a final pre-input check; bench clocks and callback order (B; PT:271-285, LC:660-672).
2. Restore/observe coupled engine state: player body, carryable links/collisions, Problem children and meaningful audio/timers (PR:483-487; CA:81-98; CR:641-673).
3. Use typed, sentinel-aware clock rebasing and frozen hearing ages; test expiry edges and a delayed verdict (GI:13-15; HE:30).
4. Validate a versioned seed protocol and global draw ordering; exercise randomness before removing its refusal (PR:420; CR:671).
5. Keep held-body/engine-on refusals until their own proof; remove the post-result far-item tolerance (CA:6-10; D:70-73/94).
6. Restrict REPAIR to action-only changes; bind two unique SAME receipts to immutable point/current protocol and enforced producer identity (WG:392-405/460-469).
7. Make every new admission rule fail closed on absent/bad records; add targeted negative controls and require the intended failure reason (D:88; WG:209-221).
8. Label the spliced saving an estimate or buy a fresh fixed baseline; count launches/pauses and keep engine proof separate within ~20 min (D:86-97).
